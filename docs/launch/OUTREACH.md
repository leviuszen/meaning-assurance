# Bounded Individual Outreach Packet — Do Not Send

## Purpose

Invite technically relevant people to reproduce the deterministic demo and
challenge the evidence boundary. Do not ask for stars, promotion, endorsements,
or positive reviews.

## Candidate scope

- Maximum first group: 10–20 people
- One individually researched message per recipient
- Eligible recipients:
  - maintainers of coding-agent workflow tools;
  - maintainers of AI code-review tools;
  - people who publicly documented coding-agent babysitting or false completion
    claims;
  - researchers or practitioners working on agent evidence, governance, or
    human-in-the-loop decisions.

## Message skeleton

```text
Subject: A small coding-agent evidence protocol related to your work on <specific topic>

Hi <name>,

I saw your public work on <specific project, post, or finding>. I am building
Meaning Assurance, a local file-backed protocol that separates a coding agent's
completion claim from frozen evidence, reviewer findings, moderation, and human
acceptance.

I am not asking for a star or promotion. If the mechanism is relevant to your
work, would you be willing to run the credential-free deterministic demo and
challenge one question: what evidence boundary is still missing or misleading?

Repository: <FINAL_REPOSITORY_URL>
Demo: powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\Run-Demo.ps1

Current limits: Windows/PowerShell only, first-party evidence case, no guarantee
of correctness, and no automatic merge.

Thank you,
LEVIUS
```

## Required authorization before contact

The user must approve:

- the recipient category and maximum count;
- the identity and contact channel used;
- the exact message;
- whether one follow-up is allowed; and
- whether any response may be quoted publicly.

No contact is authorized by preparing this packet.
